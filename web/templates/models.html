Key finding

Supervised models score about 99% in cross-validation, but the test set contains attack types that never appear in training, and the classifier misses most of them. The Isolation Forest only learned what normal traffic looks like, so it catches about twice as many unseen attacks. Combining both gives the best overall detection. That's why NetGuard combines the services into one verdict.
